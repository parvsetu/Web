Technical blueprint · October 2026

Parvsetu

A platform for festival committees (mandals) in India: festival registration, online and desk passes with single-use QR entry, volunteers at the gate, donations with 80G receipts, stall bookings for vendors, and promotion through brands, field agents and bloggers. This page explains how it is built, how each flow works and what the database holds.

Architecture

All business rules live in one API. The web app, and future Android and iOS apps, only render state and send requests; they never decide a scan result, a price or a permission.

  end

Authorization. Every request re-reads the user, their roles and status; a disabled user's sessions stop at once. A permission is granted by a mandal-wide role or a festival-only assignment. Someone with no access to a mandal gets "not found", so other mandals' festivals are never disclosed.
Images. Logos, banners and festival photos go through one storage interface. Today the bytes sit in Postgres with a per-mandal quota; moving to object storage is a new implementation, not a change for callers.
Payments. One provider interface: create a payment, then verify the gateway's signed webhook. Adding Razorpay or PhonePe is one class. Receipt and invoice numbers come from atomic counters and are assigned only once money is confirmed.

Business flows

Each flow below is enforced on the server; the diagrams show the order of events and where money and state change.

Mandal onboarding and festival go-live

flowchart LR
  A["Registration - self, logged-in user or field agent"] --> B["Email verified"]
  B --> C{"Platform review"}
  C -- "changes requested" --> A
  C -- "rejected" --> X["Closed"]
  C -- "approved" --> D["Mandal, admin login and festivals created"]
  D --> E["Festival fee pay link, one per festival, 14 days"]
  E -- "paid or waived" --> F["Festival LIVE - public, bookable, scannable"]
  E -- "paid" --> G["Field agent earns referral and commission"]
  • Each festival goes DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED_AWAITING_PAYMENT → LIVE, separate from its day-to-day status. Every public query, the booking page and the gate check it.
  • The fee is quoted at submission (mandal override, festival type, catalogue group, then default) and locked at approval.
  • A refund takes a festival back off-line only while it has no passes.

Visitor booking, payment and gate entry

sequenceDiagram
  participant V as Visitor
  participant API as API
  participant G as Payment gateway
  participant S as Gate scanner
  V->>API: Choose festival, date, slot, people (optional referral code)
  API->>API: Price from slot and peak-day rules, GST, hold places for 15 min
  API-->>V: Order PENDING with checkout
  V->>G: Pay
  G->>API: Signed confirmation
  API->>API: Lock order, mint QR passes, invoice number, settlement split
  API-->>V: Passes (one per person or one group QR)
  S->>API: Scan QR
  API->>API: One conditional update ACTIVE to USED
  API-->>S: Entry allowed, or already used / expired / not yet valid
  • Free passes skip the gateway. Unpaid orders expire after 15 minutes and give their places and held credit back.
  • Desk volunteers can also issue passes against the mandal's prepaid credit; the per-pass commission is debited atomically and a pass is refused if credit runs out.
  • QR codes carry only a random token and an HMAC signature, so forged codes are rejected before any database lookup and no personal data is in the QR.

Blogger referrals

flowchart LR
  A["Blogger signs up with channels and festivals"] --> B{"Platform approves blogger"}
  B --> C{"Platform approves each festival"}
  C --> D["Referral code and link /f/festival?ref=CODE"]
  D --> E["Visitor books through the link"]
  E -- "payment succeeds" --> F["Commission credited once"]
  F -- "every pass in the booking cancelled, or refund" --> H["Commission reversed once"]
  F --> P["Platform pays the blogger"]
  • Commission is a percentage of the ticket value before GST or a fixed amount per booking: a platform default, with an override per festival.
  • Free bookings, self-referrals (the blogger's own mobile or email), suspended bloggers and switched-off links earn nothing.
  • The platform pays commissions; mandal settlements are never reduced by them.

Donations, anonymous gifts and 80G

flowchart TD
  A["Treasurer records a donation - cash, UPI, card, bank or online"] --> B{"Anonymous?"}
  B -- "yes" --> C["No name stored; receipt says Anonymous donor; never 80G"]
  B -- "no" --> D["Name, optional PAN or other ID, encrypted"]
  C --> E["Receipt number when payment is confirmed"]
  D --> E
  E --> F{"80G eligible? decided by the server"}
  F -- "yes" --> G["Receipt shows 80G URN and deduction"]
  F -- "no" --> H["Receipt makes no 80G claim, reason shown to staff"]
  G --> I["Form 10BD sheet per financial year"]
  I --> J["Mandal files on the income-tax portal and records the acknowledgement"]
  J --> K["Form 10BE per donor from the portal, issue recorded"]
  • 80G eligibility needs: payment confirmed, not anonymous, the mandal's 80G registration verified by the platform, the donation inside its validity, cash within the limit (₹2,000), and the donor's ID.
  • Receipts are versioned: a change to what a receipt says issues a new version; a refund cancels it. Nothing is deleted.
  • A change to a donation already in a filed Form 10BD flags that filing for correction.
  • Anonymous totals per year are shown against the section 115BBC threshold for the mandal's CA. Parvsetu never computes tax.

Promotional partners (brands)

  • A brand prepays a wallet and runs campaigns that the platform approves.
  • When a pass is issued, up to two eligible campaigns print on it and the brand's wallet is charged in the same transaction. A brand short of funds is skipped; a pass is never refused because of a brand.

Mandal payouts settle everything pending in one bank transfer with its reference. Agent and blogger payouts can never exceed what they are owed.

Guarantees

Concurrency and integrity are enforced by the database, not only by application checks.

Single-use passes. One conditional UPDATE … WHERE status = 'ACTIVE' decides each scan; a partial unique index allows one successful scan per pass. Retried scans replay the stored verdict.
No overselling. Time-slot places and stalls are counted under a row lock that includes unexpired holds, and re-checked at payment.
Money credited once. Commissions and settlements are written in the payment's own transaction behind unique indexes, so a replayed gateway confirmation cannot pay twice.
Hard limits. CHECK constraints keep balances non-negative, anonymous donations nameless, and an 80G-eligible donation tied to a verified deduction and a donor ID.
Audit. Roles, members, festivals, passes, money and tax changes write an audit row in the same transaction. Sensitive values (PANs, bank accounts) are encrypted at rest and only ever logged masked.
